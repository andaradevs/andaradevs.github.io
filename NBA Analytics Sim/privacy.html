Privacy Policy

NBA Analytics Sim
Last Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

NBA Analytics Sim is an NBA statistics and game-simulation application. It does not require an account or sign-in. Simulation history and language and theme preferences are stored locally on the device; we do not maintain our own account database. When sports data is requested or ads load, the device communicates with external providers, which may receive technical connection data such as IP address and device or request information. Google Mobile Ads is integrated for banner and interstitial formats; the current configuration uses test units. RevenueCat is included as a dependency, but this version does not initialize purchases or process payments through that service.

2. How We Use Your Information

The application uses local history and preferences to display simulations and personalize the experience. It queries external sources for schedules, scores, rosters, statistics, and, when available, injury data that power simulations. Google Mobile Ads may request ads and process technical data to deliver them, prevent fraud, and measure performance; personalization options depend on the platform, configuration, and applicable consent.

3. Advertising, Purchases, and Third-Party Providers

We do not sell personal information. Google Mobile Ads (Google AdMob) provides the integrated advertising formats. Although the current configuration uses test units, releases with production ads may allow Google and its partners to process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. The specific data depends on configuration and device or regional privacy choices. RevenueCat is installed but is not initialized or used for purchases in this version; if purchases are enabled in a future Android version, RevenueCat may receive an app user identifier, transaction, receipt or entitlement information, and diagnostic data to validate and restore purchases. Google Play would process payment; NBA Analytics Sim does not receive or store card details. We will update this policy before enabling that feature. Each provider handles data under its own policies.

4. External Data Sources

The application queries NBA Stats, ESPN public services, and NBA CDN for statistics, rosters, schedules, injuries, and results. Certain schedules may use BALLDONTLIE when a server-side key is configured; if a source is unavailable, the app may fall back to another source or the local cache. Sports data powers simulations and is cached locally. Each external provider may process technical request data under its own policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not directed to children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal identification information from minors. If we discover that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

6. Data Security

We use HTTPS for communications with external sources and store simulation history, preferences, and the sports data cache on the device. We do not store card details, passwords, or session cookies. External providers are responsible for the security measures applicable to data they receive through their services.
